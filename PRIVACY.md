## English
- This app is **not a medical device**; it makes no claims about diagnosis, treatment, measurement accuracy or medication doses. The notice is shown on every screen.
- The tools work offline. Your health and wellness records (steps, water, weight, sleep, calories, blood pressure/sugar, medicine times, mood, journals) are stored **only on your device**; they are never sent to a server, sold or used for ad targeting.
- We do **not** ask for or collect accounts, names, e-mail or contacts.
- **Motion / physical activity** permission is used only to count steps in the step counter.
- **Location** is used only while a run or ride is being recorded, to measure distance; never in the background and never sent. Route traces stay on the device.
- The **camera and flash** are used only during the pulse estimate (for fun/wellness); no images are saved or sent.
- **Notifications** are used only for reminders you set (water, medicine, posture, eye breaks, bedtime, end of fast).
- What you write in the mood journal stays on the device. If crisis words are noticed, helpline information is shown on screen only; nothing is sent. The "findahelpline.com" link opens in your browser only if you tap it.
- Food values are based on USDA FoodData Central (public domain) records and our own estimates for traditional dishes; they are approximate.
- The **toothbrush timer** (for kids) has no ads, purchases or rewarded ads, and no interstitial is shown when leaving it.
- Ads are served by Google AdMob; your health data is never given to the ad network. On first launch you choose once ("Accept" / "Essential only"); where required, Google's consent form is also shown. Change it any time in Profile → Ad settings. Ads never interrupt a task.
- In-app purchases are processed by Google Play / App Store; we never see payment details.
- Profile → "Delete all my data" removes everything stored on the device.
- No gambling, games of chance or 18+ content.
